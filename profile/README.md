# Vixel AI

**Turn your story into an AI short film.**

Vixel Creator brings your story, characters, storyboard and media into one workspace. Make a children's animation, a short drama, a stylized comedy or a brand film. Add interactive choices when they serve your story.

[Start creating](https://drama.vixelai.com/creator?source=github&entry=org_profile) · [Watch the films](https://www.vixelai.com/case-studies) · [Read the guides](https://www.vixelai.com/guides)

## See a finished example

[**Why Is the Ocean Blue?**](https://www.vixelai.com/case-studies/why-is-the-ocean-blue) is a 40-second animation made by the Vixel team. Watch the finished film, explore its creative approach, then adapt a separate practice idea for your own story.

## Make your first film

1. Start with a small idea or script.
2. Review your story, characters and storyboard.
3. Generate and review individual shots, then assemble your sequence.

AI output may need revisions. Opening a practice brief does not generate media; image and video generation uses credits. [Check current plans and limits](https://drama.vixelai.com/plans).
